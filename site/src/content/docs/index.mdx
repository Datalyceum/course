## How every lesson works

<ol class="dl-steps not-content">
  <li><span class="dl-steps__n">1</span><span><strong>Watch</strong> a whiteboard video of about 10 minutes, built on real data, in English, French, Spanish or Japanese.</span></li>
  <li><span class="dl-steps__n">2</span><span><strong>Read</strong> the notes on this site: the key diagrams, common confusions and where the idea lives in Azure.</span></li>
  <li><span class="dl-steps__n">3</span><span><strong>Check</strong> yourself with a three-question quiz before and after the lesson.</span></li>
  <li><span class="dl-steps__n">4</span><span><strong>Practise</strong> with homework in three levels, Core, Stretch and Challenge, then compare with the solutions a week later.</span></li>
</ol>

## The whole course

Eight theory seasons take you from data foundations and big data to machine learning, deep learning and AI agents. Then a hands-on track opens the real Azure services, with what each one costs and how to switch it off. See the [course map](/course-map/), or get the quizzes, homework, data and notebooks on [GitHub](https://github.com/datalyceum/course).
