Learn AI with Pictures explains one AI idea in one picture. You can read each picture in about a minute, and every picture comes with a short video that walks through it, with audio in English, French, Spanish and Japanese.

The pictures stand on their own, so you can start anywhere. When one makes you curious, the full lessons in the [course](/course-map/) go deeper.
