Welcome. This is a free course that takes you from zero to a solid understanding of AI, data science and big data, and shows where each idea runs in the real world on Microsoft Azure.

## Who it's for

You're starting from zero, or you work in IT and want to move into data and AI. You don't need a maths degree. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can start here.

## How each lesson works

| Step | Where | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Watch | [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@datalyceum) | A whiteboard video of about 10 minutes, built on real data |
| 2. Read | This site | The key diagrams, common confusions and where the idea lives in Azure |
| 3. Check | [GitHub](https://github.com/datalyceum/course) | A three-question quiz before and after the lesson |
| 4. Practise | [GitHub](https://github.com/datalyceum/course) | Homework in three levels: Core, Stretch and Challenge |
| 5. Share | [Discussions](https://github.com/datalyceum/course/discussions) | Post your homework, ask questions, help others |

A new lesson comes out every week. Short videos during the week recap one idea each.

## Videos in four languages

Every lesson has audio in English, French, Spanish and Japanese, with subtitles in all four. On YouTube, choose the language under **Settings → Audio track**.

## Theory first, then Azure for real

The course has two tracks. First the theory, in eight seasons, where every lesson ends with where the idea lives in Azure. Then a hands-on track where we open those services for real. Whenever we use Azure, you'll see what it costs and how to switch it off, so you never get a surprise bill.

## Where to begin

1. Start with [Lesson 0.1: The Map](/season-0/lesson-0-1-the-map/), even if you already know some AI.
2. Subscribe on [Substack](https://datalyceum.substack.com) to get each new lesson by email.
3. Star the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/datalyceum/course) to keep track of new lessons.
