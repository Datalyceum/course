Datalyceum is a free, independent course on AI, data science and big data, with Microsoft Azure as the place where every idea runs for real.

## Who makes it

Scripts, diagrams and fact-checking are by **Sedat Salman**. The narration in the videos is AI-generated, in English, French, Spanish and Japanese.

## Where to find the course

- Videos: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@datalyceum)
- Lessons by email: [Substack](https://datalyceum.substack.com)
- Quizzes, homework, data and notebooks: [GitHub](https://github.com/datalyceum/course)
- Questions and homework answers: [Discussions](https://github.com/datalyceum/course/discussions)

Found a mistake? Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/datalyceum/course/issues/new/choose), or use the **Edit page** link at the bottom of any page.
