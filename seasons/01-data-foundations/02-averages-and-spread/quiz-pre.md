# Lesson 1.2: quiz before the lesson

**1. Five people earn 30, 32, 35, 38 and 400 (thousand a year). Which number best describes a typical person?**

- A) The mean, 107
- B) The median, 35
- C) The biggest value, 400

**2. Two classes both have an average test score of 70. What else would you want to know to compare them?**

- A) Nothing, they are the same
- B) How spread out the scores are
- C) The name of the teacher

**3. A chart that shows how many values fall in each range of a number column is called:**

- A) A pie chart
- B) A histogram
- C) A line chart

<details>
<summary>Show answers</summary>

1. B. One very large value pulls the mean up to 107, more than four of the five people earn. The median, 35, sits in the middle and ignores how big the largest value is.
2. B. Same average, different spread: one class could have everyone near 70, the other half near 40 and half near 100.
3. B. A histogram puts the values into ranges (bins) and shows how many fall in each. It shows the shape of the data.

</details>
