# Which AI model fits you?

"Which AI model is the best?" is the wrong question. The right one is: best for what? Four questions and one chart help you pick the kind of model that fits you.

Video: out Thursday, 19 November · Notes on the website: [datalyceum.com/guides/which-ai-model](https://datalyceum.com/guides/which-ai-model/)

## The four questions

1. **What will you use it for?** Everyday writing, coding, long documents, images and voice, or research.
2. **Where can your data go?** Public or personal: any app. Company data: a business plan or your company's cloud. Must never leave: an open-weight model on your own machine.
3. **What's your budget?** Free plans for learning, a subscription for daily work, pay per token for your own app.
4. **Speed or depth?** Thinking models for hard problems, small and fast ones for volume.

## The names, as of November 2026

- Closed families: GPT (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), Grok (xAI), Muse (Meta)
- Open-weight families: Qwen, DeepSeek, Mistral, Gemma, Llama, Phi, gpt-oss

Versions change every few weeks; check a live leaderboard such as [Arena](https://arena.ai/leaderboard) before you decide.

**Try this:** take three real tasks from your week, run them in two or three models, and keep the one that does them best.
